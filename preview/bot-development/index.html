04FAQ

Questions about bots.

Can the bot run 24/7?

Yes. I deploy it on a server and set it up to restart automatically, so it stays online without your computer being on.

Telegram or Discord?

I build for both. Telegram suits customer-facing bots, shops and announcements. Discord suits communities, roles and moderation. If you are unsure, describe your audience and I will suggest one.

Can the bot connect to my website or spreadsheet?

Yes. Bots talk to other tools through APIs and webhooks, so orders, leads and messages can flow into the places you already work in.

What does a bot cost?

Projects run from $10 to $10,000. A simple command bot sits at the low end, while a bot with payments, an admin panel and several integrations needs a bigger budget. Send a brief and I will quote it.

Can you fix or extend a bot I already have?

Yes. Share the code or describe what it does, and I will review it before suggesting a fix or a rebuild.
