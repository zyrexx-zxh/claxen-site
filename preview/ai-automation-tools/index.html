04FAQ

Questions about AI tools.

Which AI models do you use?

I work with the major LLM APIs and choose by quality, cost and privacy needs for your task. If a simple rule or a small script does the job, I will say so rather than sell you AI.

Will the AI make mistakes?

Sometimes. I design tools so that important outputs can be reviewed before they are sent or saved, and I test prompts on real examples to catch weak spots early.

Is my data safe?

I only send the data that a task needs, avoid storing what is not required, and talk through privacy options with you before building. Tell me early if the data is sensitive.

How much do the AI services cost to run?

Model providers charge by usage. I estimate monthly running costs during scoping and build in limits and caching to keep them predictable.

Can you add AI to my existing bot or website?

Yes. Most AI projects are an extension of something that already exists, such as a Telegram bot, a form or a dashboard.
