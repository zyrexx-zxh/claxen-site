05FAQ

Good questions.

How much does a project cost?

Projects start at $10 and go up to $10,000, depending on scope. A small script is a different job from a full web app with an admin panel. Send a brief with your budget and I will tell you what is realistic.

What do you build?

Four things: custom websites and web apps, Telegram and Discord bots, Python automations and backends, and AI-powered tools. Each has its own page with details.

How do we get started?

Message me on Telegram at t.me/claxen or email claxen@outlook.com with what you need, your budget and your deadline. The brief form on this page writes that message for you.

Can you work with something that already exists?

Yes. I can fix, extend or rebuild an existing site, bot or script. Share the code or access and I will review it before suggesting what to do.

Will my website show up on Google?

I build sites with fast loading, clean semantic HTML, metadata and structured data so search engines can understand them. Rankings also depend on content and competition, so I do not promise a position.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. I stay reachable for fixes and updates, and we can agree on ongoing work if you want it.

Do I get the source code?

You get the code and the access at launch, unless we agree otherwise at the start.
